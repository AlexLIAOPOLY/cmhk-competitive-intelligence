One input permits at most 12 repair requests and 3 requests for any one scope or candidate, including requests interrupted by a process or network failure. Protocol 6 collects all invalid groups and sends their actual draft and individual errors together. Zero-selection review uses batches of at most 30 candidates. Each batch has one normal request and at most two repairs; the whole stage permits at most 12 extra repair requests. Normal batch requests are counted separately, so larger valid inputs do not exhaust a retry budget merely by progressing. Restarting after a completed batch never calls the model for that batch again, even if the secondary payload cache was not written. Invalid drafts retain all field errors and transport evidence. A source-backed terminal sentence mark is equivalent for quote matching; interior wording and numbers must still match the candidate exactly.
